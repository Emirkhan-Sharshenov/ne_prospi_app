## Название (27/30)
Don't Oversleep: Stop Alarm

## Краткое описание (69/80)
A GPS alarm that wakes you before your stop. The screen can stay off.

## Полное описание (1756/4000)
Don't Oversleep is an alarm that goes off by distance, not by time: it rings when your bus, train, minibus or metro is approaching your stop.

Close your eyes and put the phone in your pocket. The screen stays off, the app watches the road and wakes you in time.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap a point on the map, search for it by name, or pick a transit stop.
2. Choose when to be woken: 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 1 mile away, or 3, 5, 10 minutes before arrival.
3. Press Start and rest. The alarm rings by itself.

SO YOU REALLY DON'T MISS IT
• A loud alarm over the lock screen — dismissed only by holding the button, so you can't swipe it away half asleep.
• Backup timer: wakes you after the time you set, even if GPS is lost completely.
• Warnings when satellites are lost or the battery is running low.
• If you pass the stop anyway, the alarm rings again.
• An "are you awake?" check a minute after the alarm — switch it on in settings if you tend to fall back asleep.
• Vibration, flashlight and a spoken announcement, for when the sound has to stay off.
• Sound in headphones only, so the whole bus isn't woken up.

MAP AND STOPS
• OpenStreetMap map and transit stops — anywhere in the world.
• Offline map: download your country and travel without the internet.
• Saved places, recent trips and history — two taps away.
• Home screen widget and a quick-start shortcut.

STRAIGHT TALK
• No ads, no sign-up, no tracking. Your coordinates stay on your phone.
• The app is free and the source code is open.

Languages: English, Russian, Kyrgyz. Metric and imperial units.

The app needs background permission and battery optimisation turned off, otherwise the system can stop it while you sleep. On first run it shows exactly what to enable and offers to test the alarm.
